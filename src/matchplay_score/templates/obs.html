One card at a time, sized for the player panel above your stream camera.

In OBS, add a Browser source, paste this URL, and set its size to 640 × 270. Position it in the upper-right player panel. On a 1440 × 810 canvas, use 480 × 200.

The default cycle shows recent cards, then the top three, and repeats. Recent cards includes cards in progress, ordered by their latest submitted game. Top cards uses each player's highest valid card. Voided and invalid cards stay off the stream.

Checks saved tournament data every 25 seconds. Only the coordinated publisher contacts Match Play; opening a page never refreshes the source. The current card stays visible if updates are unavailable.
